Shipping policy

Everything ships from Pune, Maharashtra, all-India.

Timeline

Every order gets a digital proof — usually within 2 hours of ordering — and nothing goes to the press until you approve it. Once approved, we print, quality-check and dispatch within 48 hours. Transit time on top of that depends on your pincode; check the delivery estimate on any product page before you order.

Cost

Shipping is a flat rate shown at checkout, waived automatically once your order passes the free-shipping threshold shown in your cart. Both figures are set by us and can change — what you see at checkout is always the current, correct one.

Tracking

Once dispatched, your order carries a courier name and AWB number, visible on My Orders. We currently hand tracking numbers over manually rather than emailing them — check that page for the latest status.

Address accuracy

We print and ship to the address on the order. If a parcel is returned to us because of an incomplete or incorrect address, re-dispatch is at the shipping cost above — double-check your PIN code and phone number before confirming.

Returns & refunds

Custom-printed goods work differently from off-the-shelf ones — here's exactly how.

Before we print

Nothing goes on a garment until you approve the digital proof, so this is the point to catch a mistake. You can request changes or cancel with no cost at all before approving.

After you approve

Once a proof is approved, printing begins on a garment cut specifically for your order — at that point we can no longer cancel, resize, or refund for a change of mind. This is standard for custom/personalised goods and matches what every print-on-demand shop operates under.

What's always covered

If what arrives has a genuine print defect, was damaged in transit, or isn't what you ordered, that's on us — a free reprint or a full refund, no argument. Reach out from My Orders → Raise a request with a photo within 7 days of delivery and we'll sort it from there.

Refund method

Any refund goes back the way it came — no store credit unless you'd genuinely rather have that.

Terms of service

The short version: design responsibly, we print faithfully.

Your designs

Anything you upload — a logo, a photo, artwork — has to be something you actually have the right to print. Don't upload someone else's copyrighted or trademarked work unless it's yours to use. We reserve the right to decline printing anything illegal, hateful, or that infringes someone else's rights.

Pricing

The price shown at checkout is the one that's charged — it's computed by our server from the current bulk-pricing tiers and tax rate at the moment you order, not a figure your browser makes up. If a price changes between opening your cart and checking out, we'll tell you rather than silently charging the new number.

Accounts

Keep your password to yourself; you're responsible for orders placed from your signed-in account. Saved designs and past orders are tied to your account, not your device.

Payment

Placing an order reserves it — we confirm payment with you directly before anything is printed. We'll update this page the day that changes to an automated checkout.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India, with courts in Pune, Maharashtra having jurisdiction over any dispute.

Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and what we don't do with it.

What we collect

Your name, email and phone number when you create an account; a delivery address when you place an order; and whatever you upload or type into the Design Studio to build your print.

What it's used for

Fulfilling your order, letting you sign back in to track it or reorder, and answering you if you contact support. Nothing here is used to build an advertising profile, and we don't have an ad business to build one for.

Payment data

We don't currently process or store card or payment details on this site at all — payment is confirmed with you directly, outside the checkout flow, until an automated payment gateway is live.

Sharing

Your delivery details go to the courier that ships your order and nowhere else. We don't sell customer data, to anyone, ever.

What's stored on your device

Just your theme preference (light/dark) and a session cookie that keeps you signed in — both there to make the site work, not to track you elsewhere.
